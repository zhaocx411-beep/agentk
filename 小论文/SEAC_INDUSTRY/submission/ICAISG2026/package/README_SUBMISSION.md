## Verified conference rules

- Conference: 20–22 November 2026, Hangzhou, China.
- Extended full-paper deadline: 15 September 2026.
- Notification: 15 October 2026; registration deadline: 25 October 2026.
- English only; full papers must be at least five pages including figures, tables, and references.
- Pages beyond six incur an overlength fee. This five-page version has no overlength page.
- The submission must be original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere.
- The conference states that review is double blind and uses two or three reviewers.
- Choose exactly one route: EasyChair or email. Do not submit through both.
- At the current post–25 July rate, the official registration page lists RMB 3,500 for a regular publication and RMB 3,300 for a student/IEEE-member publication; pages beyond six are RMB 400 each.

Official pages: [submission](https://www.icaisg.org/submission.html), [review](https://www.icaisg.org/review.html), [conference home and dates](https://www.icaisg.org/), [registration](https://www.icaisg.org/registration.html), and [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=icaisg2026).
